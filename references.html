About the PDFs

A PDF is included only when its licence allows it to be redistributed. That means CC BY, or for unmodified non-commercial use, CC BY-NC or CC BY-NC-ND. Every other paper links to the publisher through its DOI. Licences are as printed in each PDF and were checked in October 2026. Preprints are marked: they are not peer reviewed, so treat their claims with more caution.
